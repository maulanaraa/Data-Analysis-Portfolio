# 02 — Global Superstore Commercial Intelligence & Cross-Border Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
An enterprise-grade commercial decision system and international market intelligence platform analyzing multi-region transactional operations across worldwide markets (APAC, EMEA, LATAM, US & Canada).

## 🚀 Key Modules
1. **Global Executive Macro View**: Worldwide revenue velocity, operating margins, currency/regional dynamics.
2. **Cross-Border Supply Chain & Freight Subsidies**: International shipping modes, fulfillment delays, and freight loss analysis.
3. **Market Expansion & Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Global customer cohorts, retention rates, and enterprise account health.
4. **Product Profitability & Market Elasticity**: SKU-level margin leakages across global distribution hubs.
5. **Strategic Action Playbook & Prescriptive Policy Simulator**: Automated ROI interventions for country-level discount caps and logistics restructuring.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Framework**: Python 3.10+, Streamlit
- **Analytics Engine**: Pandas, NumPy, Scipy
- **Visualization**: Plotly Graph Objects / Express
- **Design System**: Pure Matte Black Canvas, Borderless Bento Grid, Satoshi Typography
