# 02 — Global Superstore Commercial Intelligence & Strategic Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview
Enterprise-grade multi-region commercial analytics and cross-border performance intelligence system for Global Superstore worldwide operations.

## 🚀 Key Analytical Modules
1. **Global Macro Trajectory**: Worldwide revenues, profit margins, currency & regional performance.
2. **Cross-Border Logistics & Freight Dynamics**: International shipping modes, shipping days, and logistics margin leakages.
3. **Customer Retention & RFM Segmentation**: Global customer lifetime value, enterprise accounts, and churn risk detection.
4. **Product Profitability & Market Elasticity**: SKU and Sub-Category profitability across worldwide distribution hubs.
5. **Prescriptive Action Plan & What-If Simulator**: Interactive policy modeling for country-level discount caps and profit recovery.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Framework**: Python, Streamlit
- **Data & Computation**: Pandas, NumPy
- **Visuals**: Plotly Dark Interactive Suite
- **Design System**: Pure Matte Black Canvas, Borderless Bento Grid, Satoshi Typography
